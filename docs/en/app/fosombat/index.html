About game

A fighting game with one unusual mechanic: every hit you land or take charges your Echo meter. When it is full, tap it and a translucent copy of yourself appears where you stood 2.5 seconds ago. It replays everything you did since — every step, punch, kick and special — while you keep fighting. Two of you against one.

The echo is invulnerable, deals reduced damage and fades when its replay ends. Land a combo, then cast the echo so your past self repeats it while you set up the next one.

What's inside: • four fighters with their own stats and signature specials; • three arenas: Neon Shrine, Ember Pit, Frost Sanctum; • arcade vs CPU with three difficulties, training, local two-player and online directly between phones; • every visual and every sound is generated by code — the game ships zero binary assets.

How to play

The joystick is on the left: move, up to jump, down to crouch. Buttons on the right: P punch (a chain of three), K kick (of two), B block (hold; down + block is a low block), SP special. Double-tap a direction to dash.

Down + punch is an uppercut launcher, down + kick is a sweep you have to block low, jump + attack comes from above.

The Echo meter sits under the health bar: when it fills, tap it and a copy replays your last moves. First to two rounds wins.

On a keyboard: WASD move, F and G attack, H block, T special, R echo. Gamepads work too.
