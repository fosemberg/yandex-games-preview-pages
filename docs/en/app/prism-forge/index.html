About game

A 3D tower defence with no tower shop. You cannot pick a tower here — you can only forge one out of gems.

The tray at the bottom is always full: five stones, and a slot freed by a recipe is refilled at once. Gold is not spent on getting a stone but on refusing one you do not need. Three stones make a recipe, and the recipe becomes a tower on the field.

Four things make this a game rather than a lottery: • the level of a stone is part of the recipe: three rubies make a Spark, three higher rubies make a Forge — a different tower entirely; • repeating a recipe is how a tower levels up: there is no upgrade button, just build the same recipe again on your own tower; • drop quality is for sale: Refinement shifts the distribution upwards, and that is the main strategic choice of the match; • a move is a wave: exactly one thing goes on the field per turn, so a whole match gives you about twenty towers, no more.

How to play

Watch the tray at the bottom. As soon as the five stones contain a recipe, the ones you need start glowing and its name appears above the tray — tap the hint and the whole set is selected. You do not need to memorise a table of twenty-two rows.

With a set chosen, tap a free cell and a tower goes up there. The same recipe placed on your own tower levels it up.

If nothing fits, place a lone stone: a shard hits at about half strength, but it holds a cell and buys time. And it is not a dead end — take two stones from the tray, tap the shard, and it becomes the third ingredient of a real tower on that same cell.

You can discard and refine as much as you like, but you may place only once per turn. The wave walks the path to the core; let an enemy through and the core loses a life.
