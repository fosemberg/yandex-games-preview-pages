About game

A turn-based space RPG. Wells have opened across the galaxy — holes that swallow a ship and spit it out who knows where, and the Hollow climb out of them. Five orders argue about what to do: recluses seal the wells, harvesters live off them, pilots chart them, zealots dive into them, builders want one of their own.

You are a pilot. Trade, take government contracts, hunt pirates, hand condensate to the Guild for skill points — or plug the wells with it and push back the day the Mouth opens.

What's inside: • a galaxy of 14 systems generated from a seed — the same seed always gives the same galaxy; • a living world: traders fly, pirates rob, patrols cruise, planetary economies drift every day; • a spaceport where you can see where you landed, with a greeter who addresses you according to their order; • eight tech levels of equipment, eight goods, smuggling and customs; • real-time combat in hyperspace tunnels; • two endings.

How to play

The game runs in days. Give your orders — course, landing, attack, jump — and press Start: a couple of seconds of real time, ships fly, shoot and dock, and the calendar moves one day. Orders outside a turn cost no time.

Tap empty space to plot a course, pinch to zoom; the course shows where the ship will be at the end of each day. Three buttons at the bottom: autopilot, speed 1–3× and Start. The autopilot hands control back on landing, on an event or when an enemy shows on radar.

On a planet there is a hangar, a government, an equipment shop and a trading hall. On stations you hand in condensate, earn ranks and join the builders' programme.

If you are ambushed in a hyperspace tunnel the ship starts listening to the stick and the right button holds fire: clear the tunnel or run for the edge.
