About game

A turn-based card strategy on a 5×4 board. Every battle starts with a purchase phase: spend 30 coins on the cards offered in the shop, place your squad on your half of the board, then lead it into combat — move, shoot, cast abilities and destroy every enemy creature.

What's inside: • over 1000 cards with unique art — warriors, archers, mages, beasts, buildings and heroes; • dozens of mechanics and whole schools of cards: runes, crystals, the silt of the White Grove, the webs of the Void; • offline draft — pick 20 cards (1 of 4 each time) and fight up to 10 wins; • a collection, a deck editor and recommended deck sets; • daily quests, achievements and boosters; • a bot with five difficulty levels and seven personalities.

How to play

Tap Play — the first battle is a tutorial, then the difficulty rises on its own.

Purchase. You start each battle with 30 coins. Pick cards in the shop and place them on the two bottom rows: melee fighters up front, archers and healers behind. When the coins run out, the battle begins.

Battle. Sides take turns. Tap your card to see where it can move and whom it can attack. An attack is a coin flip between a weak and a strong hit; armour and shields reduce the damage. Many cards have abilities with cooldowns — their buttons appear under the board.

Goal: destroy all enemy creatures. If nobody takes damage for several rounds, death mode kicks in, so a battle always ends.
