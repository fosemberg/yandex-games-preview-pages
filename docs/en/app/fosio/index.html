About game

A board game for 2–6 monsters about a fair in the fairy-tale city of Brontograd. The city is a ring of districts, each with three dice on its street, a banner and a stall with one item. On your turn you step into any district and take all its dice: swords hit your neighbours and storm banners, coins pay at the stall, mana casts your ability.

What's inside: • 7 districts with their own bonuses, 12 abilities, 25 items and 11 herald's news that change the rules of a round; • banners pay glory at the end of every round, and the first to 18★ wins; • a knockout never eliminates anyone: the monster rests in the infirmary until its turn; • play against bots or pass one screen around, or online with friends by a link; • two views: cards — district tiles with dice, flags and stalls — and a 3D fair town with shadows; • match statistics: glory, damage and coin charts, and awards.

How to play

At the start of a turn your monster gets 1💧. Pick a district — with a button at the bottom or by tapping the map — and step there: you take all three dice from the street and the district bonus.

Swords ⚔️: one sword is one damage to a monster in your district or a share of storming a banner. An empty banner costs 1⚔️, someone else's 2⚔️, and a guarded one 3⚔️. Coins 🪙 buy the item at your district's stall, mana 💧 pays for your ability, ✚ heals.

At the end of a round each of your banners gives +1★, and standing on your own banner gives another +1★. From the second round the leader moves first, and the herald announces news that changes the rules.

The game ends at the end of the round in which someone reaches 18★. The full rules are in the main menu.
