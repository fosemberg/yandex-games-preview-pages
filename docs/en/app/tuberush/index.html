About game

Arcade racing inside a giant pipe. The track is a closed loop and you can drive all 360° of it: floor, wall, ceiling. A ramp throws the car towards the axis, while gravity pulls it to the nearest wall — a strong jump flips you over the axis and you land upside down.

What's inside: • nine track themes — sewer, steampunk, ice, toxic lab, hive, lava, neon, space and tomb, each with its own grip and gravity; • six car bodies and eight kinds of auto-aiming turrets: machine guns, shotgun, rockets, mines, plasma, laser, railgun; • a garage with a 3D stand, paint and a turret layout; • obstacles you have to read: pipe slaloms, crossbars, swinging maces and corridors that drag the car sideways.

How to play

Right thumb is a virtual stick: left and right steer around the circumference of the pipe, down brakes. Throttle is automatic and can be switched off in the settings. Left thumb holds the two weapon-group buttons and nitro.

A group button lights up red when its turrets have a target: only machine guns can afford to fire into empty space — they overheat, while rockets and the railgun run out of ammo.

On a keyboard: A/D steer, W/S throttle and brake, Space and K fire, N is nitro.

Races pay credits: spend them in the garage on turrets and upgrades, or in the shop on a new body. Tracks unlock one after another.
