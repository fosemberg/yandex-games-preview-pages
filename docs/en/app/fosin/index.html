About game

The world has flooded, and divers race to −1000 m. Whoever breaks through the bottom first wins… if they beat the Bottom Boss knocking from below.

What's inside: • a sonar row instead of a blind deck: three face-down cards whose backs hint at the danger, and you choose where to ping; • Locals with character — a crab housing committee, a squid from accounting, an anglerfish life coach — and Hazards at every step; • roles, mutations, companions and gear that comes in sets; • rope contracts: a rival helps you in a fight on fair terms, and once per game you can cut the rope; • Pranks to throw at a neighbour at the worst possible moment; • a new Current every round changes the rules; • bots with three personalities and two-player games by a link or a QR code; • a 3D sea floor and a 2D "sonar console" mode for weaker phones.

How to play

A turn is a dive. First gear up: put on gear, play a mutation, a companion or a Prank. Then ping one of the three sonar cards — the marks on its back hint at how scary it is.

Met a Local? Fight: your Power against its Threat. Call a buddy on a contract, hire a Day Diver or fight alone while the neighbours throw cards in. Lose, and you toss bait or panic — or your catch net tears.

After a win, decide: go deeper (the Threat grows, but so does the loot) or surface with what you have. You can sink no more than one depth per turn.

The Bottom Boss waits at −1000 m: three trials, win two and the bottom is broken. Beginners get a tutorial with instructor Borya in the main menu.
