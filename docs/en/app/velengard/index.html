About game

An open-world action RPG in the spirit of classic medieval hack'n'slash. The world is 2000×2000 tiles and 67 zones, from level 1 to level 200. At its centre lies a hand-made heartland: the village of Velengard, a lake, goblin meadows, the Wolf Forest, the ruins of Caldor, an orc camp and the Dragon Plateau. Around it spirals sixty more zones, each three levels meaner than the last.

The space between is not empty: the whole map has a climate — snow, ash, marshes, deserts — and every block rolls dice for a landmark. Packs roam between them, and trails wind through, worn down by somebody who turned back.

What's inside: • seven classes with their own skill trees; • a second world to choose — the Endless March, a map with no end; • towns with residents, traders and waystones you can travel between; • day and night, fog, shadows, wind in the canopy; • two-player co-op directly between devices, no account and no server.

How to play

Create a hero: name, gender, world — hand-made Velengard or the Endless March — and one of seven classes. After that the world is open.

Move with the virtual joystick on the left. Tap an enemy to attack, the buttons on the right are abilities with cooldowns. Gestures zoom and rotate the camera. HUD panels can be dragged wherever suits you and the extra ones hidden.

Follow the road: it links the zones in order, easy to hard, so it is hard to get lost in levels. Take quests from residents, look into camps and ruins on the way, attune waystones — you can travel between attuned ones.

Death does not send you home: the hero wakes up where they were walking a minute ago.
